## Verified claims used in slides/caption
- Fine-tuned lightweight Gemma on local scam conversations (impersonation of institutions/banks/figures, regional schemes)
- On-device: neither Singapore Government nor Google can access users’ conversations through deployment
- User consent required when integrated into apps
- Scams = over half of Singapore’s reported crimes; more than S$4 billion losses since 2019; social-engineering scams up to 80% of losses
- Two-stage pipeline: (1) lightweight classifier screens messages, (2) fine-tuned Gemma analyzes larger conversation snapshot
- Real-time on-device warning to pause before sharing sensitive info/funds
- Phased rollout with Singapore Government; exploring similar tech for other countries
