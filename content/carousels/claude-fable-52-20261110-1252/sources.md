# Sources — Claude Fable 5.2 price cut & launch offers (Anthropic)

Fetched 2026-11-10 ~12:45–12:55 KST.

- Anthropic announcement (official, Nov 9, 2026): https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2
  - Fable 5.2 & Mythos 5.2 = same underlying model; Fable has cyber/bio safeguards and is available to all users; Mythos via Life Sciences / Cyber Verification Programs
  - Improvements in coding, reasoning, vision; outputs up to 30% faster than 5.1
  - Price $8 / MTok input, $40 / MTok output; fast mode up to 2.5x speed at $16 / $80
  - Recommendation: start most coding/knowledge tasks with Claude Opus 5.5, switch up to Fable/Mythos as needed
  - Availability: Fable 5.2 today on all platforms incl. AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure; model id `claude-fable-5-2`
  - FrontierCode 2.0: Fable 5.2 High effort ≈ Opus 5.5 Max (45.3% vs 45.5%) for roughly half the cost per task (not on slides)
- Pricing page (official): https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing
  - Fable 5.2: $8 input, $10 5m cache write, $16 1h cache write, $0.20 cache hits, $40 output
  - Fable 5.1: $10 input, $0.25 cache hits, $50 output → 20% cut on input/output/cache-read
- Fable 5.1 overview (official): https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/fable-5-1/overview — $10/$50, now Legacy
- Launch offers (official Help Center): https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17506494-claude-fable-5-2-launch-offers
  - Pro plans & standard Team seats: all Fable models included Nov 9 – Nov 16, 2026 11:59 pm PT, up to 50% of weekly usage limits, plus one Fable reset (expires Nov 16 11:59 pm PT)
  - Max, Ultra, premium Team seats, premium legacy seat-based Enterprise seats: 1–3 resets by tenure (<6 mo: 1; 6–12 mo: 2; >12 mo: 3), expire Mar 8, 2027 11:59 pm PT
  - Not available on Free plan, standard legacy Enterprise seats, self-serve/usage-based Enterprise, HIPAA-ready Enterprise
  - Resets only for accounts with paid billing before Nov 9, 2026 10:00 am PT (= Nov 10 03:00 KST)
  - Reset restores weekly usage spent on Fable; refills 5-hour limit fully; does not raise limits; cannot be undone
  - Default model doesn't change — choose Fable in model picker; Fable uses limits faster than other models
  - Use: Settings > Usage > Limit resets > "Reset for free"
- Limit reset article (official): https://support.claude.com/en/articles/17007452-what-is-a-limit-reset
- Press context: CNBC https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/anthropic-claude-fable-5-2-mythos-5-2.html ("cost 20% less than the 5.1 generation")
